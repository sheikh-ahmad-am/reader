Reader

Reel Counting System

Three counting rooms in one app. Reader reads Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels from your own Google Sheets and writes back the exact counts — sheet-ready, row by row, in the same order as your sheet.

⬇ Download for Android
Version 1.29 · No sign-in — install and start counting

The three rooms

Reel Reader — Instagram Reels
YT Reader — YouTube Shorts
FB Reader — Facebook Reels

What it counts

Views · Likes · Comments · Shares — exact numbers, plus author followers where the room supports it.

How it works

  1. Install — open the downloaded file; Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser, tap Allow.
  2. One switch — turn on the app's Accessibility switch once (Settings → Installed apps → Reader) so it can read reels for you.
  3. Pick a room and press start — paste your Google Sheet link, choose your rows, and the app opens each reel, reads the counts, and saves your results file.
