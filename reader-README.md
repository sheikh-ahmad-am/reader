# Reader

**Reel Counting System** — three counting rooms in one app.

Reader holds three rooms under one roof — **Reel Reader** (Instagram), **YT Reader** (YouTube Shorts) and **FB Reader** (Facebook Reels). Each room reads its reels from your own Google Sheets and writes back the exact counts: **Views, Likes, Comments, Shares** — plus author followers where the room supports it. Results stay sheet-aligned, row for row, ready to paste back.

### How it works

1. Install the app and turn on its Accessibility switch once (Settings → Installed apps → Reader).
2. Pick a room, paste your Google Sheet link, choose your rows, press start.
3. The app opens each reel, reads the counts, and saves a sheet-ready results file.
